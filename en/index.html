Call for Papers

We are looking for technical talks with real experience and a clear point of view.

The event will be made only of talks. We want useful proposals for people designing, building or maintaining applications with Avalonia: architecture, performance, UI, tooling, deployment and production stories.

01

Architecture

MVVM, composition, navigation, modularity and applied patterns.

02

UI and product

Interface design, theming, accessibility and polished experiences.

03

Performance

Startup, rendering, memory, profiling and practical optimization.

04

Cross-platform

Desktop, mobile, browser, packaging, deployment and CI/CD.

05

Migrations

Lessons from moving from WPF, Xamarin.Forms, WinUI or .NET MAUI.

06

Ecosystem

Open source, libraries, tooling and practices that scale.

Every talk follows the Code of Conduct

Online format

45-minute talks, precise pacing and an experience designed for remote attendance.

The day is organized into two talk blocks, morning and afternoon. There will be a 5-minute break between talks and a central pause from 14:00 to 15:00.

01

Talks only

No workshops or panels: every session is technical and 45 minutes long.

02

A deliberate rhythm

Five minutes between talks to pause, switch context and prepare for the next session.

03

Online first

The remote format brings together speakers from anywhere in the world while making every talk easy to follow from wherever you are.

Community

A meeting point for the Avalonia ecosystem.

Avalonia Conf exists to give visibility to community projects, practices and lessons learned. One day focused on applicable content, technical judgment and conversations between people building with .NET.

Key information

What matters for now.

When is it?

Wednesday, December 2, 2026, online, from 09:00 to 17:00 CET.

How do I attend?

It is free and needs no registration: the event is broadcast on an open live stream, and the link will be published on this page before December 2.

Can talks be submitted already?

Yes. The Call for Papers is open until November 13 at 23:59 CET, and proposals are submitted through the Koliseo form.

How long are the talks?

Every talk will be 45 minutes. There will be a 5-minute break between talks.

Which talk languages are accepted?

Talk proposals and sessions are accepted in Spanish and English. Each session's language will be shown in the schedule.

Is there a code of conduct?

Yes. Attendees, speakers and organizers all follow it in the chat, in talks and on social media, and it explains how to report any problem.
